Useful information

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00. Check-in is flexible: let us know your arrival time and we'll arrange it.

How do I get here from Bari Centrale station?

On foot, it's an easy walk of about 25 minutes. Alternatively, direct trains run from Bari Centrale to Brigata Bari station in about 5 minutes: the guesthouse is a 5-minute walk from the stop. Check the timetable on the website of Ferrotramviaria.

How do I get here from the airport?

Take bus 16, or the direct train from the airport to Brigata Bari station, a 5-minute walk from the guesthouse. Check the direct train timetable on the website of Ferrotramviaria.

Is there parking?

We don't have private parking, but there's free on-street parking nearby and a few paid private garages.

Is breakfast included?

No, we don't serve breakfast. The shared kitchen is always available for guests to make their own.

Is there a tourist tax?

Yes, the City of Bari tourist tax is €2 per person per night, for the first 4 nights.

Which floor are the rooms on? Is there a lift?

The rooms are on the first floor and the building has no lift: you reach them by a flight of stairs.

Are the rooms cleaned every day?

No, daily cleaning is not provided. Your room is cleaned and made up before you arrive.

Are the rooms quiet?

The windows are not soundproofed, so you may hear street noise. For light sleepers, we provide earplugs in the room.

Can I work during my stay?

Yes: guests can use the building's coworking space for free, Monday to Friday from 10:00 to 18:00. Every room also has a desk and chair so you can work comfortably.

About us

The people behind LoStabile

LoStabile opened its doors in February 2024 after the building renovation. It is a project designed and run by LAN Architetture – Impresa Sociale: a cooperative of architects who believe in hospitality built on beautiful, functional and welcoming spaces.

The project was funded by the Apulia regional programme PugliaSociale IN – Imprese Sociali and by the City of Bari programme “Un Negozio Non è Solo Un Negozio”. Besides the guesthouse and the coworking space, LoStabile also hosts a makers lab and LAN's architecture studio.
