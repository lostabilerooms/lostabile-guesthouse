What data we collect

This website is for information only: it has no contact forms, requires no registration and uses no analytics or profiling tools.

  • Browsing data. The hosting service (GitHub Pages) automatically records technical data such as IP address, browser and pages visited, for the operation and security of the site.
  • Email. If you write to us, we use your address and what you tell us only to reply and to handle your request or your stay.

Bookings

Bookings are made on external platforms: our direct booking page (bookpage.io), Airbnb and Booking.com. The data you enter there is processed according to those platforms' policies. The data needed for your stay (for example for the guest registration required by law) is processed by us to meet our legal obligations and kept for the period the law requires.

Cookies

This website does not use its own cookies, nor any analytics or profiling cookies. Fonts are loaded from our own site, with no external services.

The map in the Location section is provided by Google Maps and only loads if you press “Show map”. Google may then set cookies and receive data such as your IP address, in accordance with Google's privacy policy. If you don't press the button, the map is not loaded.

The “Check availability” form on the home page is provided by CiaoBooking (Smartness S.r.l.) and is loaded from cdn.ciaobooking.com, so your IP address is shared with that service. The form does not set any cookies on our site. When you start a search you are taken to our booking page (lostabile.bookpage.io), which uses technical cookies and, only with your consent through its banner, analytics and marketing cookies, as described in its cookie policy.

Links to Instagram, Airbnb, Booking.com and bookpage.io lead to external sites, which have their own cookie policies.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its processing or to object to it, by writing to lostabile.rooms@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).
